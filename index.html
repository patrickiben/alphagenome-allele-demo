Is the ClinVar allele the odd one out at its own site?

At each of 120 ClinVar-selected coordinates in DNM1, PCCA, PCCB and MMUT, three substitutions are possible. For every Atlas scorer the pilot asks whether the clinically annotated one sits farther from a background profile than the other two, against a null that shuffles which substitution counts as the ClinVar one. Change the slice below and the test reruns in your browser.
